    Welcome to Kypedia

    It is not a general encyclopedia. It is one writer's collection, kept in encyclopedia form because that form is good at what this needs to do: hold long pieces, keep their sources attached, and let any paragraph be linked to directly. Start from a collection, a tag, or the full index.

    How this library is put together

    More ›

    Each entry opens with a lead paragraph that answers the question in the title, then works outward through numbered sections, quoting sources in place and footnoting them at the bottom. Headings are permanent: once published, a section keeps its anchor so links from elsewhere keep pointing at the right paragraph.

    Nothing is hidden behind a search box. Collections are the shelves, tags are the cross-references, and the index shows the whole shelf list at once.

    Reading the labels

    Entries are published before they are finished, so each one carries a maturity label. Trust them accordingly.

    Adding to the library

    New writing is added by copying the article template into articles/ and registering it in library.js. The full specification — required metadata, section conventions, citation style, tag rules — is in AGENTS.md, which is written for both people and coding agents.
